Description of the Course

This course is the second semester of a two-semester sequel on quantum theory. Topics to be covered include the following:
Quantum measurement theory, statistical-density operators, wave functions and the Schrödinger equation, time-evolution
in the Schrödinger, Heisenberg, and interaction pictures, connections between classical and quantum mechanics, angular
momentum and spin, spherical wells, identical particles, scattering theory and the hydrogen atom. Approximation methods covered
include: Time-independent and time-dependent perturbation theories, variational principle.
